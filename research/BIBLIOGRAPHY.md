## Data Synthesis Notes

### Force-to-Power Conversion Methodology

**Formula**: P = F × v (Power = Force × Velocity)

**Velocity Estimation Sources**:
1. Motion profiles from literature (position vs time)
2. Typical SPM data (strokes per minute)
3. Stroke length specifications

**Validation**:
- Cross-reference with energy efficiency percentages
- Compare calculated peak power to tonnage ratings
- Verify cycle energy matches published data

### Typical Values Synthesis

From multiple sources, established reasonable ranges:

| Parameter | Low | Typical | High | Source |
|-----------|-----|---------|------|--------|
| Cycle Time | 15 sec | 20-25 sec | 40 sec | Multiple |
| Peak Force Duration | 2 sec | 3-5 sec | 8 sec | AHSS, MDPI |
| Energy Savings | 15% | 20-30% | 60% | Industry |
| Press Capacity | 100 ton | 200-300 ton | 800 ton | Manufacturers |
